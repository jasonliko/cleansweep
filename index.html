How it works

Three steps.
One cleaner wall.

01

Design the stencil

We turn your logo, message or artwork into a reusable stencil sized to the wall.

02

Spray the cleaner

A plant-based, biodegradable cleaner loosens grime only where the stencil is open.

03

Rinse and reveal

A low-pressure rinse lifts the dirt away, and the clean surface becomes the art.
